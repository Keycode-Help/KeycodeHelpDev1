🚀 Force Clear Browser Cache

This page will help you completely clear your browser cache to fix the Analytics 403 error.

Method 1: Hard Refresh

Press Ctrl+Shift+R (Windows) or Cmd+Shift+R (Mac)

Method 2: Clear Storage

Method 3: Manual Steps

  1. Press F12 to open DevTools
  2. Right-click the refresh button (while DevTools is open)
  3. Select "Empty Cache and Hard Reload"

Method 4: Complete Browser Clear

  1. Press Ctrl+Shift+Delete (Windows) or Cmd+Shift+Delete (Mac)
  2. Select "All time" for time range
  3. Check "Cached images and files"
  4. Click "Clear data"
✅ After clearing cache, you should see:
"🚫 Analytics completely disabled - no imports loaded" in console

❌ You should NOT see:
Any 403 errors from va.vercel-scripts.com
